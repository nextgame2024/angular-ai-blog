Sophia AI

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026

1. Who this policy covers

This policy describes how Sophia AI handles personal information when you visit our website, contact our team, use our business services, or communicate with Sophia AI through WhatsApp. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean Sophia Ai.

Where Sophia AI provides software to another business, that business determines how it uses its customers’ information. Its privacy notice also applies. This policy specifically includes enquiries sent to Sophia AI’s own WhatsApp number, +61 400 388 532.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact and account information: names, email addresses, phone numbers, business details and account identifiers supplied when contacting us or using our services.
  • Conversations: messages, enquiries, responses, attachments where supported, conversation history, and information you choose to provide. Our team may record follow-up notes and the status of an enquiry.
  • WhatsApp information: WhatsApp identifiers, profile or display names provided by Meta, message identifiers, timestamps, delivery status and the business phone number involved.
  • Business integration information: when an authorised administrator connects Meta or WhatsApp, relevant business account identifiers, phone number identifiers, granted permissions and access credentials needed to operate the connection.
  • Technical information: connection and device information made available to our services, authentication events, error reports, and operational or security logs.

Information comes directly from you, authorised business users, and connected services such as Meta/WhatsApp. Please avoid sending passwords, payment card details, government identifiers, or sensitive personal information in ordinary chat unless specifically required through an appropriate process.

3. How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, provide requested services, manage accounts and conversations, generate relevant AI-assisted answers, route enquiries to our team, maintain integrations, troubleshoot errors and protect our services. We may also retain information needed to resolve disputes or meet applicable legal obligations.

Providing a phone number or sending an enquiry does not by itself give permission for unrelated marketing. You can ask us to stop promotional contact.

4. AI-assisted conversations

Sophia AI may send your message, relevant conversation context and approved business information to an AI service to generate a response. The WhatsApp integration uses OpenAI API services to generate responses; it does not fine-tune a model as part of answering your enquiry. AI responses can be inaccurate; ask for a team member if you need clarification or human assistance.

AI service processing, training practices and provider retention depend on the service and account configuration. You may contact us for information about the providers used for your service. Providers may retain operational or security records under their own service terms.

5. Who receives information

Relevant information is available to authorised team members and service providers needed to deliver the service. For the WhatsApp integration, these include Meta/WhatsApp for messaging, OpenAI for AI responses, Render for backend hosting, Amazon Web Services (including Amplify) for website hosting, and Neon for database hosting. Providers receive information relevant to their role; an AI response provider does not need an administrator’s integration credentials.

Other features, such as voice or avatars, may require additional providers. Their use and associated disclosures must be explained when those features are enabled. We may also disclose information when legally required, or as necessary to investigate misuse and protect rights and safety.

6. Where information is handled

The current deployment includes Render backend services in Singapore, AWS Amplify website hosting in Northern Virginia, United States, and AWS resources in Sydney, Australia. Messaging and AI providers may process information in the United States and other countries under their service arrangements. Website hosting in one region does not mean all conversation data is stored in that region.

Staff access to customer information is currently limited to Australia. We are planning further migration to AWS in Australia. This is a future plan, not a promise that all current data processing occurs in Australia.

7. Security and storage

We use access controls and technical safeguards designed to limit access to authorised users and protect information handled by our services. No system can guarantee absolute security. WhatsApp messages received by our business integration are processed by our systems and relevant providers so that we can respond; they are not confined to the WhatsApp application on your phone.

8. Retention and deletion

Retention depends on the purpose of the record, the service configuration and applicable legal requirements. Conversation records, security logs and backups can have different retention periods.

You can request deletion using the instructions below. We may need to verify your identity and clarify the records involved. Where a record must be retained for a legal obligation or unresolved dispute, we will explain the limitation. Deletion from active systems does not necessarily remove an existing backup immediately or delete records held independently by Meta or another business.

9. Access, correction and privacy requests

To request access, correction or deletion, or to make a privacy complaint, contact hello@sophiaai.com.au. Include your name, the account email or WhatsApp number involved, and a description of your request. Do not send passwords, access tokens or identity documents with your initial request.

We will assess your request, arrange proportionate identity verification where needed, and explain the outcome or any limitations. If a different business controls the relevant records, we may direct you to that business. If you are dissatisfied with our handling of an Australian privacy complaint, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, where applicable.

Read the data deletion instructions.

10. Website storage and external services

Our application uses browser storage or cookies as needed for functions such as sign-in and preferences. Connecting a Meta account involves Meta’s own services and privacy practices. Third-party websites linked from our services have their own privacy policies.

11. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change and identify the effective date. Material changes may also be communicated through the service or directly to affected users where appropriate.
