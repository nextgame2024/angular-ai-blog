Request deletion of your Sophia AI data

Effective date: 19 November 2026

  1. Email hello@sophiaai.com.au with the subject “Sophia AI data deletion request”.
  2. Identify the email address or WhatsApp number associated with your interactions and explain whether you want a particular conversation, your account, or all applicable personal information deleted.
  3. We may ask for proportionate verification of your identity before acting. Do not send passwords, Meta tokens, or identity documents with your initial request.

We will explain the scope of deletion, any information we need to retain, and the applicable timeframe. Backups and provider-held records may follow separate deletion schedules. If another business controls the information, we will explain how to contact it.

Disconnecting a Meta or WhatsApp integration stops the connection but does not itself erase previously stored records. Request deletion separately. Deleting information held by Sophia AI does not delete your WhatsApp account or messages independently retained by other participants.
